You do not need a title, a stage or a million followers. Start with one useful thing, close to home.

A FEW WAYS TO BEGIN

01

Help a patch of nature.

Join a local clean-up or habitat restoration group. Choose native plants and help care for them after planting.

02

Support people nearby.

Ask a verified community kitchen, food bank or shelter what supplies they actually need before donating.

03

Make room for a real conversation.

Listen across differences. Challenge prejudice without dehumanising people. You can be firm without being cruel.

04

Check before you share.

Find the original source, compare credible reporting and correct a mistake when you discover one.

05

Ask for accountability.

Use lawful, peaceful channels to ask public bodies for clear information about decisions that affect your community.

06

Make less waste.

Repair, reuse, share and avoid buying things you do not need. Small habits matter more when they spread.
