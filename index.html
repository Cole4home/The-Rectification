A NOTE FROM THE PEOPLE WHO CARE

This is not a perfect-world fantasy or a fan club for a single leader. It is a place to say that cruelty is not inevitable, that the natural world is not disposable, and that ordinary people deserve a say in the future they will inherit.

We can be different without being enemies. We can question power without worshipping another kind of power. We can disagree, check the facts, and still show up for each other.
