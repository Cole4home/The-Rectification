A FIELD NOTE: THE LIVING WORLD IS NOT BACKGROUND DECOR.

START WITH FOUR QUESTIONS

A / ENVIRONMENT

What are we leaving behind?

Look at air and water quality, habitat loss, waste and climate impacts in your region. Local data is often more useful than a viral graphic.

B / HUMAN DIGNITY

Who is being left out?

Access to food, shelter, education, safety and equal treatment are not abstract ideas. Learn how rights are defined and protected.

C / ACCOUNTABILITY

Who has the power?

Ask who made a decision, who benefits, who carries the cost and what evidence is publicly available. Separate documented facts from allegations.

D / INFORMATION

Can this claim stand up?

Check the original source, publication date, context and independent reporting. If a post demands instant outrage, slow down before sharing.
